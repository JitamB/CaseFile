Note from the finance systems group, copied here because it affects how the
regional numbers read. From the February close, net revenue is reported after
discounts rather than on gross invoiced value. The change was agreed in December
and applies from the 1st. Any month-on-month comparison that straddles the
boundary will show a step of roughly three to four per cent that is entirely the
definition and not the business.
