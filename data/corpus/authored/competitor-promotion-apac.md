headline: Meridian Cloud opens aggressive APAC switching offer
---
Meridian Cloud has begun offering twelve months at half list to customers migrating
from incumbent platforms, with the offer restricted to its APAC territories.
Analysts read it as a share grab ahead of the company's regional listing. The
promotion does not extend to European or Indian enterprise accounts, and Meridian
has said publicly that it has no plans to widen it this financial year.
